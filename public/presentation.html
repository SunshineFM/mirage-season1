[01 — DesertDrop Thesis] Mirage is the season-1 evidence of the DesertDrop thesis — that the Coachella Valley should not just consume AI tools from elsewhere; it should build its own. Most social networks are built to last forever. This one was built to disappear, then get archived. No accounts means no friction. No profiles means no performance. No algorithm means no anxiety. You show up, post, read, leave. The passphrase was the same for everyone in the Valley during festival season. That was the whole mechanic.

02 — What Mirage Proved

season 1: the honest scorecard

what worked
  • AI video ads — reached real people, generated traffic spikes
  • Coachella Bound — set the season's tone before launch
  • Good Tips — highest engagement of any feed during weekends
  • No-account posting — zero friction onboarding, people just posted
  • Passphrase mechanic — local identity without accounts or profiles
  • Farewell page — a designed ending that people appreciated
  • Agent monitoring — caught bugs and regressions in real time
what failed
  • Not enough QR codes — the physical distribution layer was too thin
  • No ambassadors — no one on the ground at the festivals
  • Weak physical activation — no presence where the crowd actually was
  • Broken flows after deploys — some users hit dead ends mid-weekend
  • Ad disapproval — Meta rejected several creatives, limiting reach
  • Platform friction — passphrase confusion for some new arrivals
agents made building cheaper. they did not make reality disappear.
[02 — What Mirage Proved] The honest list — what works and what doesn't is the most cited section of the case study. What worked: the ads, the soundtrack, the tips feed, the no-account mechanic, the passphrase, the farewell page, and the agent monitoring loop. What failed: physical distribution, ambassadors, post-deploy stability, Meta ad approval. The biggest lesson is that software got easier. Attention still requires humans on the ground, relationships, and physical presence. That doesn't change just because the app was built by agents.
